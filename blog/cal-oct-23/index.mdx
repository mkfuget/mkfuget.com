Took a couple trips to California in Fall of 2023 first to San Francisco then to San Diego to visit my friends. 
These were my first times diving outside of the Puget Sound so it was exciting to see new enviromnents.

My favorite parts of these trips were the kelp forest in Carmel, the seals at La Jolla Cove and this octopus I caught sneaking into
a lobster trap and stealing a lobtster.
