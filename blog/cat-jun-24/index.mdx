Taking a couple trips to Casino Point on Catalina Island while I am in LA. Diving in California is always a nice experience for the kelp 
forests. Giribaldi are also always around but the highlight of this trip is definitely the giant black seabass. Huge creatures probably the biggest 
sea creature I have seen to this point, but they are so calm and unassuming they just sit still in the water column.
